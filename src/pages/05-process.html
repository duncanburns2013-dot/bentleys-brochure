The selling process

You'll likethe way we work

  1. 1 Preparing your property

    We walk the house with you and agree what is worth doing and what is not. Then professional photography, floor plans and copy — before a single buyer sees it.

  2. 2 Setting the price

    A comparable analysis built from MLS PIN data for your street and your buyer, not a county average. We will tell you what we think it is worth even when that is not what you hoped to hear.

  3. 3 Going to market

    Launch across the MLS, remax.com, our own audience and the Newburyport.com channel on the same day, so the first week — the week that matters most — is fully used.

  4. 4 Reporting your progress

    Showing feedback and traffic, as it happens, with a recommendation attached. You should never have to ask us how it is going.

  5. 5 Closing the transaction

    Offer strategy, negotiation, inspection and financing, through to the deed. Our transaction coordinators do this every week.
