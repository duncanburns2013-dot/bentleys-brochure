Pricing strategy

The right pricegets the best result

A house priced correctly on day one draws the most interest it will ever draw, from the buyers most able to act. That first fortnight is not a rehearsal — it is the whole of your leverage.

Priced too high, the same house sits, and the market reads the sitting as a signal. The reductions that follow arrive after the audience has already moved on, and the final number is usually lower than the honest one would have been.

We build your price from MLS PIN comparable sales, current competing inventory and what we are seeing in our own showings this month — then we tell you what we think, plainly.
