Built here,on purpose

Robert Bentley and Alissa Christie founded Bentley's in Newburyport in 2016 with eight agents. Robert had already been the region's top individual agent since 2011. Alissa came from practising law, and brought that discipline to how the office handles a transaction.

The office is veteran-owned and woman-owned. It has grown to around sixty agents and staff across Massachusetts, New Hampshire and Maine, with managing brokers in each state, and it has been the number one brokerage in Greater Newburyport every year since 2019.

We joined RE/MAX because it gave our agents a global network without asking them to answer to anyone outside this market. The decisions about your listing are made here.
